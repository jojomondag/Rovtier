# Art Style Guide

## 1. Visual Pillars
*   **Fantasy Hyper-Realism:** Grounded textures and lighting, but with fantastical elements.
*   **"Gunpowder & Scales":** The core aesthetic clash between feudal Japanese technology and prehistoric biology.

## 2. Character Guide
*   **Silhouette:** Distinct shapes for each class to be recognizable at a distance.
*   **Materials:**
    *   **Liquid Metal:** Used for special armor or magical elements.
    *   **Iridescence:** Scales and feathers should shimmer and change color based on lighting.

## 3. Environment
*   **Scale:** Massive trees, giant cliffs, and tiny humans compared to the dinosaurs.
*   **Materiality:** Mud, blood, rust, and wet surfaces. High fidelity textures.
