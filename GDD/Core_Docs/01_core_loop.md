# Core Loop & Objectives

## 1. "Nest Wars" (PvPvE Extraction)
The core game mode is **Nest Wars**.
*   **Objective:** Infiltrate the map, secure Dinosaur Eggs from wild nests or enemy bases, and extract them back to your Matriarch.
*   **The Twist:** You are not just extracting loot; you are stealing the future generation of your faction.

## 2. The Matriarch (Living Base)
Your base is not a building; it is a **Titan Dinosaur** (The Matriarch).
*   **Mobile Base:** The Matriarch moves slowly across the map or stays in a defended position.
*   **Defense:** Players must defend their Matriarch from enemy raiders.
*   **Upgrades:** Feeding the Matriarch resources unlocks new abilities and buffs for the team.

## 3. The Hatchery (Meta-Game)
Extracted eggs are brought to **The Hatchery**.
*   **Incubation:** Eggs hatch into new playable dinosaurs or resources.
*   **Breeding:** Combine genetic traits to create stronger dinosaur variants.
*   **Progression:** This is the long-term progression system, allowing players to unlock new classes and loadouts.
