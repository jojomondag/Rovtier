# Gameplay Mechanics

## 1. Combat System
*   **"Wallbang" Penetration:** Different materials (wood, paper, stone) have different penetration values. Heavy weapons can shoot through cover.
*   **Ballistics:** Projectile drop and travel time for matchlock weapons.

## 2. Visceral Damage
*   **Dismemberment:** Explosives and heavy melee attacks can sever limbs.
*   **Bleeding:** Hits cause bleeding trails that can be tracked.
*   **Physical Injuries:** Leg hits slow movement; arm hits affect aim.

## 3. Locomotion & Riding
*   **VR Interaction:** Physically climb onto your dinosaur.
*   **Riding:** Control the beast with reins and voice commands.
*   **Dismount:** Seamlessly jump off for on-foot combat.

## 4. "Golden Axe" Mechanics
*   **Stealing Mounts:** You can knock an enemy rider off their mount and steal it.
*   **Taming:** Wild dinosaurs can be temporarily tamed in the field.

## 5. Symbiotic Tactics
*   **Raider & Mount:** The rider provides ranged support; the mount provides melee power and mobility.
*   **Abilities:** Combine rider gadgets with dinosaur innate abilities for unique combos.
