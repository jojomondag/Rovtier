# World & Setting

> **📖 For the deep lore and history of this world, see [The Great Fracture](file:///c:/Users/Josef/Documents/Github/Ny mapp/Rovtier/GDD/Lore/01_the_great_fracture.md)**

## 1. Concept: "Gunpowder & Scales"
**The Era:** A reimagined "Sengoku Jidai" (Warring States Period).
**The Twist:** Dinosaurs are the primary beasts of burden and war machines. Western traders have just arrived, bringing matchlock guns.

## 2. Visual Aesthetic
*   **East meets West:** Samurai armor mixed with Portuguese explorer gear.
*   **Dino Armor:** O-yoroi lacquer plates, silk sashimono banners, thick ropes.
*   **Environment:**
    *   Misty Bamboo Forests.
    *   Burning Castles / Fortresses.
    *   Vibrant Cherry Blossom Groves.
    *   Muddy Rice Paddies.

## 3. Tone
*   **Cinematic:** Kurosawa-inspired composition.
*   **Gritty:** Mud, smoke, and scales. Not a cartoon.
*   **High Contrast:** Bright blood on white snow/pink petals.
