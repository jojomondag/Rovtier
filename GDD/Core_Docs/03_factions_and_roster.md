# Factions & Roster

## 1. Matriarch Factions (The Bases)
Each team defends a unique Matriarch type:
*   **Smuggler:** Stealth-focused, mobile base. Hard to track.
*   **Shrine:** Magic/Buff-focused. Provides strong team auras.
*   **Fortress:** Defense-focused. Heavily armored, slow moving.
*   **Warlord:** Offense-focused. Can attack other bases directly.

## 2. Dinosaur Classes (The Roster)
Playable dinosaur classes with distinct roles:
*   **Ronin Raptor:**
    *   *Role:* Scout / Assassin.
    *   *Style:* Fast, high damage, low health. Good for flanking.
*   **Shogun T-Rex:**
    *   *Role:* Tank / Heavy Assault.
    *   *Style:* Slow, massive health, devastating bite. The frontline breaker.
*   **Ninja Pteranodon:**
    *   *Role:* Aerial Support / Recon.
    *   *Style:* Flight, verticality, dropping bombs/traps.
*   **Ashigaru Triceratops:**
    *   *Role:* Support / Siege.
    *   *Style:* Defensive shield, battering ram charge. Protects the team.
