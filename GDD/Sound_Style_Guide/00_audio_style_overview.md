# Sound Style Guide

## 1. Audio Pillars
*   **Dota Clarity:** Distinct sounds for every ability and action. Gameplay first.
*   **Hunt Tactics:** Sound is information. Footsteps, breathing, and reloads reveal enemy positions.
*   **Primal Weight:** Dinosaurs sound heavy, dangerous, and guttural.

## 2. Systems
*   **State-Based Vocals:** Dinosaurs have different vocal sets for Idle, Alert, Combat, and Pain states.
*   **Dynamic Ambience:** The world sounds change based on time of day and proximity to danger.

## 3. Sound Design
*   **Weapons:** Black powder explosions should be loud, bass-heavy, and smoky.
*   **Foley:** Armor clinking, leather creaking, and heavy footsteps in mud.
